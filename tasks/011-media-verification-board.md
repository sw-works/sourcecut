# Task 011 — Media Verification + Research Board

## Goal

Turn evidence into media requirements, verify candidate assets, and assemble the first production research board.

## Scope

- evidence-derived asset requirements;
- local media search;
- Gemini visual inspection when needed;
- verification against evidence;
- historical relationship labels;
- rights filtering;
- board response schema;
- evidence drill-down.

## Acceptance criteria

Canonical Bitterroot prompt produces a complete board and downgrades/rejects at least one unsupported or interpretive asset.
